## ✦ ABOUT

A small corner of the digital cosmos.

I build things because an idea appears and I want to see where it goes.

Mostly exploring **AI, Obsidian, automation, digital tools, and random experiments**.

No fixed roadmap.

Just curiosity, weird ideas, and the occasional  
**"what if I tried this?"**
